1. Introduction: The Blind Spot of Conventional Finance (The Clock-Time Illusion)

Over 99% of financial market participants and machine learning practitioners approach markets using calendar time: 1-Minute (M1), 15-Minute (M15), 1-Hour (H1) candles... Yet foundational market microstructure theory establishes that financial markets do not move in calendar time; they evolve in response to information flow and capital velocity.

⚠️ The Dangerous Blind Spot Between Two Opposite Regimes:
• Quiet Overnight Session (Asia 03:00 GMT): Only $50 million turns over across 60 minutes. Price is virtually stationary, yet the system forces 60 separate 1-minute bars into memory. Models are deceived by spurious oscillator crossovers, bleeding capital to bid-ask spreads and commissions.
• Macro Shock Event (NFP / BoJ / FOMC 13:30 GMT): $10 billion turns over in the exact same 60 minutes, driving explosive multi-pip moves. Conventional systems cram this entire micro-liquidity battleground into a single candle, reacting too late and forfeiting alpha.

The Marcos López de Prado Solution (Advances in Financial Machine Learning): Discard calendar time and align with the market's true Information Clock. A bar closes at the exact sub-millisecond when a pre-determined dollar volume threshold $D$ has transacted. When the market is inactive, zero bars form (eliminating phantom trades); during volatility bursts, dozens of bars form within seconds, capturing true price discovery at ultra-high resolution.
